How the AI works with you

  • Read-back first. Before building or doing anything, the AI tells you what it understood. You correct it or say go ahead.

  • Honest checks. Every result says what was checked, how, and what nobody checked yet.
  • Your words stay yours. The AI never silently changes what you wrote. Every suggested change is shown as before and after, and you accept or reject each one.
