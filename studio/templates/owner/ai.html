How the key is kept safe:

  • Anthropic checks the key before it is saved.
  • It is stored encrypted with the site's secret key (STUDIO_SECRET_KEY). It is never shown again, not even to you: only its last 4 characters.
  • It is never sent to anyone's browser, and members never see it.
  • Saving or removing it needs your password.
  • Keep the site's secret key private, and away from copies of the database: with both, the key could be read.
  • As a second safety net, set a monthly spending limit in the Claude Console (platform.claude.com) too.
