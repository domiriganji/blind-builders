There are two ways to pay for the AI. With your own Claude API key, you pay Anthropic's prices directly and this site charges nothing. With credits, you pay the AI price plus {{ markup_percent }} percent, which pays for running the site.

Way 1: your own Claude API key

  • You create a key at console.anthropic.com and add billing there.
  • Anthropic bills you for exactly what you use, at their prices. This site adds nothing.
  • Your key is stored encrypted. The site only ever shows its last 4 characters.
  • You can remove it at any time.
