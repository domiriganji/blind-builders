How peer checks work

  1. You give the web address and the task to try, for example: find the opening hours and book a table.
  2. Up to 5 members try it with their screen reader and write what they heard first, whether they could do the task, and the problems they found.
  3. Each check is saved in a public record with a fingerprint. If anyone changes an entry later, the record page says so.

Rewards

You can offer a reward in credits for each check. The whole amount is held from your credits when you ask. Each checker gets their reward the moment they send their check. If you close the request early, the rest comes back to you. Read how paying works.

What a peer check does not cover

A check is one member's honest report of one try. It is not a full accessibility audit, and it does not say how the page looks to sighted people unless a checker says so.
