About the fingerprints

Each check has a fingerprint, a SHA-256 hash of everything in the check, the request's name, address and task, and the fingerprint of the check before it. Changing any entry, or removing one in the middle, breaks the chain, and this page says so. On its own the chain cannot show whether the newest check was removed, and someone able to rewrite the whole database could recompute every fingerprint. If you saved the last fingerprint, compare it with the one above: if it is still there, nothing before it has changed.
