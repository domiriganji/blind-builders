Who wrote what

  • Written by the AI: the prior work summary, the study plan, the interpretation of your data and the draft paper. The AI can be wrong. Open the sources and check them.
  • Computed by the site: the counts, averages, smallest and largest values in your data file, and the check that every citation number in the draft matches a source.
  • Not checked by anyone: whether each source really says what the AI says it does. Only you, or a peer, can check that.

Each AI step uses your own Claude API key or your credits. Read how paying works.

Why this section exists

In a 2026 study of 57 blind and low-vision researchers, nearly one in five could not do a literature review on their own with the tools they had. Source: Khan and Seo, "I Don't Trust Any Professional Research Tool", CHI 2026. Read the paper's page.
