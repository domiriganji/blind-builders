Built around how you work

  • Every page works without JavaScript, on low-cost phones and slow connections.
  • Each page has one main heading that says where you are or what just happened. Use your screen reader's headings list, heading keys or rotor to move around.
  • Each page starts with a short menu: "Menu: all sections" takes you to the list of sections at the end of the page.
  • Slow AI work runs in the background. You get a page that says what is happening, and a "Check again" link. If JavaScript is on, the page checks by itself. When the work is done, it plays three short taps and moves your focus to a link to the result, so you get the news with or without sound.
  • The three taps are a rhythm on one note, not a tune, so they work with many kinds of hearing loss. If you cannot hear them, you miss nothing: focus moves to a link to your result, in speech, in braille or on screen.

Help comes from the community

Members answer each other's questions in Ask the community, check each other's work in Peer checks, and share guides in Learn. There is no phone line.
