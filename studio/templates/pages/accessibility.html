Accessibility

This site is built first for people who use screen readers, braille displays or magnification. We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA on every page.

What we test

  • Every page is checked automatically for labels, headings, link and button names, language and zoom.
  • Pages work with JavaScript turned off.
  • Nothing streams or updates by itself while you are reading. The one exception is the page that waits for slow AI work: with JavaScript on, it checks by itself and shows the current step, and says the step when it changes. When the work is done, it plays three short taps and moves your focus to a link to the result. The link reaches you in speech, in braille or on screen, even with the taps off. You can turn the taps off on that page.

What we know is not perfect yet

Some screen reader and browser combinations have not been tested by people yet. If something does not work for you, ask in Ask the community and say which screen reader, braille display or magnifier, browser and device you use. Members answer when they can.
