## The short version

- Every page has one main heading that says where you are or what just happened. Jump to it first.
- Each page puts the summary first and the details after, under headings.
- Slow AI work happens on a job page. It has a "Check again" link. If JavaScript is on, it checks by itself and plays three short taps when the work is done.
- Before the AI builds something new or does a task for you, it reads back what it understood. You correct it or tell it to go ahead.
- The AI is paid for with your own Claude API key or with credits.
- There is no phone line. Help comes from other members in [Ask the community](/help).

## Moving around a page

Every page here has the same parts, in the same order:

1. A "Skip to main content" link. It is the first thing the Tab key reaches.
2. The list of sections, in a navigation region called "Sections".
3. Your account links, in a navigation region called "Your account".
4. The main content. It starts with the page's only level 1 heading.
5. A footer with links to how the site works, how paying works, accessibility and Ask the community.

The fastest way around is by headings:

- NVDA and JAWS on Windows: in browse mode, press H for the next heading, Shift+H for the previous one, and 1 to jump to the level 1 heading.
- VoiceOver on a Mac: press VO+Command+H for the next heading.
- VoiceOver on an iPhone: turn the rotor to Headings, then swipe down for the next heading.
- TalkBack on Android: set the reading controls to Headings, then swipe down for the next heading.

The level 1 heading always matches the page title your screen reader announces when the page loads. If something went wrong with a form, the page title ends with "please fix the problems", and a list of the problems comes first. Each problem is a link that takes you to the field.

## When the AI is working: the job page

AI work can take from half a minute to a few minutes. Instead of making you wait on a page that changes while you read it, the site gives you a job page.

- Its heading starts with "Working on it", followed by what is being done.
- It says how long ago the work started and what the current step is.
- It has a "Check again" link. Activate it to see if the work is done. When it is done, the link takes you straight to the result.
- You can leave the page. Everything you started is listed in [Your recent work](/jobs).

If JavaScript is on, the job page also checks by itself every few seconds. When the work is done, it plays three short taps and moves your focus to a link that starts with "Done. Open the result". The taps are a rhythm on one note, not a tune, so you do not need to tell pitches apart. To turn the taps off, uncheck "Play three short taps when it is done" on the job page. The site remembers that choice in this browser.

Nothing streams. The answer appears whole, so it never talks over your screen reader.

If the work fails, the heading starts with "This did not finish", followed by the reason in plain words, and a link to go back and try again. Your work so far is kept.

## Read-backs: the AI checks with you first

Before the AI builds something new or does a task for you, it tells you what it understood: a read-back. Listen to it carefully.

- If it is right, tell it to go ahead.
- If something is wrong or missing, correct it in your own words. It reads back again.

When you ask for a change to something already built, there is no read-back: the AI makes the change, and the result page tells you what changed.

The AI never silently changes your words. When it suggests a change to something you wrote, it shows the text before and after, and you accept or reject each change.

Every result also says what was checked, how it was checked, and what nobody has checked yet. Text written by the AI is always labelled as written by the AI.

## Paying for the AI: your own key or credits

There are two ways to pay for the AI's work.

- **Your own Claude API key.** Connect it on [your account page](/account). The AI then runs on your key and you pay Anthropic directly. This site charges nothing for it. The site stores your key encrypted and never shows it again in full: only its last 4 characters, so you can tell which key it is.
- **Credits.** If the site has its own key turned on, you can buy credits instead. Each piece of AI work costs what the AI used, plus a markup. [How paying works](/pricing) explains the details.

If you have neither, the page tells you plainly. Nothing is faked.

Only ever paste an API key on your account page. Never put one in a question, an answer or a message to the AI.

## Where to get help

There is no phone line and no support staff. This site runs itself: the AI does the work and members help each other.

- [Ask the community](/help): ask a question and other members answer. While you wait, you can ask the AI for a suggested answer. It is labelled "Suggested by the AI. Not checked by a member."
- [Peer checks](/checks): ask other blind members to test your website or app with their screen reader.
- The other guides on the [Learn and help](/learn) page.

When you ask a question, say which screen reader, browser and device you use. The question form fills these in from your account, and you can change them. Never post passwords, one-time codes, card numbers or API keys. Everyone can read the community pages.
