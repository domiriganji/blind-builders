## 9. Measurement, gates and safety (Lena)

### 9.1 Rules for every number
- Every rate prints its numerator, its denominator and its bound. A rate without them stays a blank with its proposer's name (round 5).
- Every bound is Clopper-Pearson exact. This convention reproduces every figure in the transcript *(stated in review)*:
  - "At most 1 in N" claims use the one-sided 95% bound: 0 of 20 gives 1 in 7; 0 of 50 gives 1 in 17; 59 clean, or 93 with one failure, gives 1 in 20.
  - Every other bound, the kill's "whole interval" included, is an end of the two-sided 95% interval: 3 of 3 allows 29%; 20 of 20 bounds above 83%; 24 failures in 150 kills.
- Every gate is stated as a count, so nobody recomputes it on the day.
- Cells under five people print as counts only *(proposed in review)*.
- The weekly dashboard is generated automatically and published at 09:00 WAT every Monday, before anyone reviews it. That way the builder learns its numbers when the public does (Section 10) *(proposed in review)*.

### 9.2 Masked checks: the second instrument
- **Which pages:** every seller's first page, and the first change she publishes each calendar month (round 5). That is at most two a month, the consent cap. All fifty January first pages are masked.
- **Who and how:**
  - The masked witness did not check that page.
  - She uses a reader, phone and browser outside both the harness matrix and that page's own witness check.
  - The queue mixes in catalog and Gemini Canvas pages from Sam's phone arm, so she cannot tell which builder made a page (round 4).
  - She does the customer's task: she places the order and types what she heard.
- **What drand decides** *(proposed in review)*:
  - The drand round recorded at publish picks the witness, her reader-phone-browser combination, and an order time within 72 hours.
  - The rule for which pages is public, so drand does not hide pages. It makes sure nobody can arrange who checks, or when.
  - Section 10's "drand picks masked pages, so nobody knows them in advance" is replaced by this.
- **Sending numbers** *(proposed in review)*:
  - Orders go from signer-issued numbers: a second SIM with WhatsApp Business on her phone, or a device-lab phone.
  - They never go from a witness's own number.
  - The message opens "TEST order from Countersign. Please don't fill it." The council approves that wording.
- **Failure:** she cannot place the order within 10 minutes (Sam's customer cap, round 5), or the item or price she heard or sent differs from the approved spec *(definition proposed in review)*.
  - A failure is a Sev 2 disputed line.
  - A wrong price or a wrong number is a Sev 1.
- **Declines:** a seller who declines is left out of the headline and guardrail denominators, and is counted. Her record says "not in the outside check" (round 5).
- **Masking rate (Q8)** *(proposed in review)*:
  - The rate is an output of this rule, not a parameter.
  - January is budgeted from the rule's count. Later months use the higher of three in ten and the observed rate.
  - Lena's "one in five" was an audit assumption, not a rule.
  - The witness lead re-counts January capacity against the rule by Dec 18.

### 9.3 Dashboard

**Weekly**

**Alone and working (the headline).**
- **Definition:**
  - Base: per calendar month, sellers who published a first page or a change that month and did not decline masked orders.
  - Numerator: those whose first publish that month (the masked one) she marked "no sighted help," and whose masked order succeeded (round 5).
  - The calendar month replaces "last 30 days" so the denominator matches the masking rule *(proposed in review)*.
- **The publish question:** "Did anyone who can see help with this change? No, a little, or yes." Only "No" counts. It is never shown to customers *(wording proposed in review; the council approves)*.

- **If missed:** if the upper bound is below half on Mar 31 (39 or fewer of 100), no grant, paper or outreach says sellers build alone until it is above half *(proposed in review)*.

**Guardrail.**
- **Definition:** failed masked orders, over all masked orders on pages whose record had no open failure line on the order path. It prints with its interval and its coverage, as "covers X% of live pages."
- **Kill:** decided on Mar 31 only (9.4).
- **Weekly looks before Mar 31:** if the two-sided 99% interval is wholly above 1 in 10, the kill fires early. That is 12 of 50, 19 of 100, or 26 of 150 *(proposed in review)*.

**Seed catch rate.**
- Reported by reader, by reader version and by check class, over each reader's last 200 seeds.

- A bound bootstrapped over witnesses prints beside it, because the 200 trials come from ten witnesses *(proposed in review)*.

**Holds.** The count, plus the median and maximum hours to rule. Every hold over 24 hours is listed with its reason. Appeals are decided within 7 days.

**Desk and first session.** These are Yusuf's targets (Sections 3 and 4.1), and they stay his. The desk is reviewed weekly and the first session monthly *(cadence proposed in review)*.

**Banner.** The builder's share of signer income, on a trailing three months, recomputed monthly *(window proposed in review)*. It prints above 15%, from January.

### 9.6 Seeds and witness cutoffs

**Template battery for Dec 18** *(proposed in review; every seed is a failure named in the transcript)*.
- "Order" shown on screen but named "Send request";
- a picture she did not supply;
- an order button that needs script;
- "Puff-puff, twelve pieces, extra pepper" overflowing a card at 360 px;
- contrast under 4.5:1;
- an unlabelled Order button;
- a seven-item list without a heading;
- an order link to an unconfirmed number;
- one plant per refusal class;
- one page dressed as a bank.

**Admission.**
- A seed type enters a reader's queue only after three trusted witnesses on that reader produce its expected transcript (Yusuf, round 4). Where the reader logs its speech, the device lab fixes the expected transcript first.
- A new reader version, or a weekly rewording, counts toward catch rates but not toward suspension until it has been re-validated. A reader update must never suspend an honest witness *(proposed in review)*.

**Rates.** One seed in three for a witness's first thirty checks, then one in ten. Seeds are paid like checks.

**Cutoff** *(proposed in review)*. The transcript's "six of the last ten" assumes honest misses run near one in ten. Two hundred trials cannot pin that: 20 misses in 200 still allows 15%. So each reader's cutoff comes from the upper end of its calibration interval. That keeps an honest witness at the 15-checks-a-week ceiling under 6 suspensions per thousand witness-years, which meets Yusuf's "under one in a hundred a year."

| Honest misses in 200 calibration seeds | Suspend at (of the last 10 seeds) | Paid retraining at | Non-listener suspended within |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | 5 | 3 | about 5 seeds |
| 4-11 | 6 | 4 | about 6 |
| 12-19 | 7 | 5 | about 7 |
| 20-28 | 8 | 6 | about 8 |
| 29 or more | Reader fails the Nov 30 gate | — | — |

**What the table means at typical load** (about 22 checks a month):
- Honest suspensions run about two per thousand witness-years or fewer.
- Retraining reaches 6 to 9% of honest witnesses a year.
- A witness who listens only half the time takes 14 to 44 seeds to reach suspension (at cutoffs 6 to 8), so retraining is the earlier net.

**Voiding.** A suspended witness's lines void back to her last caught seed. Anyone who relied on them gets a free re-check.

**Witness lead.** She holds the seeds and writes the weekly rewording, which the device lab validates. While she holds the seeds she does no seed-scored checks *(proposed in review)*.

### 9.7 Safety (replaces Section 4.3 and Section 11's refusal list)

**Refused before publishing, by computed check (round 5).**
- There is no per-page override, because the council cannot veto a safety refusal. A misfire is fixed in the rule within 7 days, with two-person approval, and the desk helps her rephrase meanwhile *(proposed in review)*.
- The refusals:
  1. Any field, or any text *(proposed in review)*, asking a reader to type or send a PIN, password, one-time code, card number, CVV, BVN or NIN.
  2. Any page collecting or holding other people's money: donations, savings groups, investments, "returns."
  3. Any claim to cure disease, blindness included (Kuriyan and colleagues, NEJM 2017).
  4. Any order link to a number that has not confirmed a code.
- Word lists in English, Pidgin and Yoruba (including ajo, esusu and adashe) are built with native speakers and signed by the language designer *(proposed in review)*.

**Held for the duty reviewer.**
- Names, logos or colours matching a bank, telco, government agency, charity or disability organisation. "MTN recharge sold here" passes. Colour holds only together with a protected name or logo *(proposed in review)*.
- Prescription-only medicines *(proposed in review)*.

**Limits.**
- A new number publishes at most three pages in 30 days.
- There is no order database.
- Witnesses never solve CAPTCHAs, log in, or act on anyone's account.

**Duty reviewer.**
- A rota of three, at least two of them blind, rules within 24 hours.
- **Who is on the rota** *(proposed in review)*: from Jan 4, both blind desk agents and the part-time sighted image reviewer. From April, the desk lead replaces one of the agents.
- **Image and logo holds:** the image reviewer's description is recorded as a human DIRECT line before anyone rules.
- **No ruling at 24 hours:** the hold stands, the seller gets an SMS and a desk call, and the miss is shown on the dashboard *(proposed in review)*.
- **Appeals:** an appeal of a hold goes to three council members, who decide within 7 days. Computed refusals are not appealable.

**Battery** *(proposed in review)*.
- At least 20 planted cases per class and per hold type, across the three languages, run on every release and every template. Zero misses allowed.
- Before Dec 18, 100 real price lists from Yusuf's groups run through the checks, and every false hold is fixed. After launch, false holds print monthly with their bound.

### 9.8 Consent, privacy and research ethics (replaces Section 11's "Consent and privacy")

**Sellers.**
- Before her first build she hears four headings, of at most four items each (round 5). Proposed wording, for the council by Dec 18 *(proposed in review)*:
  - **What we make:** your page; its record; your Friday changes.
  - **What we keep:** your number; your words for 30 days; your page while it is live, and its record for 12 months after; how you use your phone, only if you tell us.
  - **Who checks:** a model company's computer in the United States reads your words; a blind checker listens on a phone; a sighted checker looks at your photos; up to two TEST orders a month, which you can refuse.
  - **Cost:** nothing; who pays is named on your record.
- **"Checked, with the count"** prints both counts, for example "Checked: 12 lines. Never checked: 3." *(proposed in review; the council approves the wording)*.
- **Nothing customer-facing says she is blind.** There is no badge unless she adds one. Her page carries no visible or spoken Countersign mark unless she switches it on, because in January the mark alone would out her. The record stays verifiable by its link *(proposed in review)*.
- **Who listened:** "a blind person has listened" prints only on a version with a witness line. Any other version opens with "No person has listened to this version; last heard [date]" *(proposed in review)*.
- **Access mode** is asked only as an optional question, with explicit consent. It is sensitive data, stored apart from page and record, never shown, and used only for headline cells *(proposed in review)*.
- **Under-18s** may build only with a guardian's consent, taken on a desk call and logged. They never witness and never take part in research *(proposed in review)*.
- **Desk voice notes** are kept 30 days *(proposed in review)*.

**Witnesses.**
- The work agreement is separate from research consent. Declining research changes neither work nor pay.
- They are told seeds exist and are paid like checks, but never which checks are seeds.
- They are named only by choice: two name switches, both off by default, and a new pseudonym on every record.
- Masked orders are sent from signer-issued numbers *(proposed in review)*.
- Sessions stop at thirty minutes.

**Testers and study participants.**
- Consent says plants exist, but not where (Priya, round 5). The same rule covers the study's seeded customer questions *(proposed in review)*.
- Research consent is by teach-back, for Lena's study as for Count Us, because research must test understanding *(extended in review)*. The product itself uses readback, never teach-back.
- Participants are paid in cash, and all are adults.
- Data are de-identified before analysis. The linkage key is destroyed 12 months after the results. Public files suppress cells under five *(proposed in review)*.

**Count Us.**
- Consent is by teach-back. Participants are paid in cash, whether or not they finish.
- Nobody shares a PIN or login with us, and nobody presses confirm.
- We keep typed transcripts, never banking screenshots.
- An accidental confirm is refunded the same day, fees included *(proposed in review)*.
- Banks get the results 30 days before the preprint, with a printed right of reply and no veto. Participants hear the results no later than the banks *(proposed in review; this replaces Section 10's "participants hear results first")*.

### 9.9 Accessibility of Countersign itself (launch gates)

**Braille-only.** On Nov 16, two braille-only testers, one of them deafblind, finish onboarding, a build and the record on a braille display, with no sound. Any failure is retested by Dec 11 *(proposed in review)*. After launch they test monthly, and on any release that touches onboarding, records or alerts.

**Zoom.** Reflow at 320 CSS px is the desktop test, so these are added *(proposed in review)*:
- pinch zoom is never disabled;
- on the lab's Tecno and Itel, at the largest system font and 200% browser text, the five core tasks show no clipped text and no sideways scroll;
- two low-vision testers over 50 who use magnification pass onboarding, a build and the record before Dec 18.

**Alerts.** No two alerts differ only in pitch. Each has its own on-off rhythm, and each also arrives as a text line *(test proposed in review)*.

**Recall and lists.** Typed digits work everywhere a value is recalled. No default list runs past four items without a heading.

**Time.** No step expires in under 20 minutes. SMS codes stay valid for 30 minutes. A reload or a power cut returns her to the same step *(proposed in review)*.

**The record (round 2).**
- The verdict comes within the first five words of each heading.
- Each heading makes one claim.
- Evidence lines come in a fixed order.
- The map names its frame ("At 360 pixels wide: ...") and uses her own visual words, through per-language colour tables.

**Will not build (round 2).**
- a vOICe- or EyeMusic-style soundscape of the screen;
- a spatial-audio page map.
