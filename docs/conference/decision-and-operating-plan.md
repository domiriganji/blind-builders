# Decision and Operating Plan: Countersign

*The chair's integrated record, October 12, 2026, drawn from rounds 1 to 5 and the judge panel. All figures are the panelists' own. "Not decided" marks a blank the panel left open, followed by its owner. Naira are converted at ₦1,500 to $1, Sam's planning rate.*

## 1. The decision

**Product: Countersign.** One website opened from a WhatsApp link, plus a phone number that a blind person answers. Paid blind witnesses are at its centre. It has two doors, opened in order:
- **"Make my page"**, the phone door (called Ready until round 5), opens January 4, 2027. Yusuf retired the old name: "a door called Ready says the barred word on every page."
- **The paid door** (paste any URL or add a CI step) opens January 11 for blind developers and agencies buying blind witness checks.

Inside the signer sit **Canary**, a free bulletin of screen-reader results for AI agent releases, and **Upkeep**, a pay-per-green-month price plan. **Count Us** is a separately funded study, not a door: a blind lecturer tests whether 100 blind Android users can reach the confirm screen of a ₦1,000 transfer alone at Nigeria's ten biggest banks, with a preprint due June 30, 2027. Receipts carry the signer's name, because "built for blind sellers" outs a builder (Lena).

**Promise.** Before you share it, a blind person has listened. You hear who checked it, on which phone and reader, and what nobody checked, in words you can repeat to customers. Anyone can verify the record is unaltered.

**First users.**
- **Kemi**, a composite of three women in Yusuf's Lagos groups:
  - glaucoma at 41, now 52;
  - sells small chops and cakes;
  - Tecno Spark on prepaid data, with TalkBack;
  - English on screen, Yoruba and Pidgin with customers;
  - no card, no laptop.
- **Sellers:** Yusuf's eleven start January 4; fifty in Lagos and Ibadan by January 31.
- **Paid door:** Priya's ten developers (her example: congenitally blind, 34, NVDA and VoiceOver, Portuguese and English), plus agencies facing the European Accessibility Act.

**Form: a website plus a human phone line, with no native app in version one.**
- Live regions are untested under TalkBack (Priya's concession), so answers load whole under new headings and nothing streams.
- A URL opens in TalkBack, Jieshuo and WhatsApp with no store and no card.
- Pages work with JavaScript off, under 60 KB, for Opera Mini and Phoenix.
- Meta's terms bar assistant bots, so there is no WhatsApp bot.
- Native only where testers find the web fails.

**Why it is not a duplicate.**

| Closest | What it lacks |
|---|---|
| The niece: Yusuf's "nearest duplicate," since sighted relatives typed all three catalogs among his eleven sellers | She owns the edit |
| WhatsApp Business catalog: free, and its cart sends orders to her chat | Untested under TalkBack; first asks for a photo she cannot check; silent about the next reader |
| Gemini Canvas: a free g.co link | Gemini wrote the page, so it cannot say "everything here is yours"; shared data sits in "a public database" |
| Fable: paid testers with disabilities | Findings belong to whoever paid, and two clients are model vendors |
| JAWS Inspect, guidepup, ariawalk-action, buyable, axe | Logs, CI gates and scans with no approved spec, seeded witness or portable record; axe "never hears" |
| Accessibility statements | Audacity 4's was "completely misleading" (#12461) |

What is new is the combination:
- checks derived from what she approved;
- a reader's speech typed from recall by a paid blind witness;
- seeded queues and masked test orders;
- two lines no builder can say about its own page: "Everything on this page, words and pictures, is something you said, sent or approved" and "Changed: small chops, twelve hundred to fifteen hundred. Nothing else changed."

**Vote.**
- The judges scored Countersign 27.7, Ready 27.0 and Count Us 24. They advised; all four panelists chose Countersign.
- The science judge's shape took three first preferences: Lena, Priya, and Yusuf, who added the blind-user judge's witness-network centre.
- Sam ranked an amended venture shape first.
- "The signer never builds" was dropped, because it "leaves Kemi no builder if October 30 fails" (Priya).

**Dissent and concessions.**
- Lena's dissent carried. In January Kemi hears "checked," with the count, never "ready," because "'ready' is 'good' in one word."
- Yusuf conceded the receipt "was mine, not Kemi's."
- Sam: the agency line "can pay wages; it cannot carry the product." He and Lena call this a grant-funded year.
- Before October 30, Sam would route sellers to free tools and Lena would build: a week's difference.
- Yusuf's voice-note desk opens January 11; Lena has not answered.
- Priya calls two companies run by the same five engineers "one company with two letterheads"; Sam counts them as independence.

**Not yet served, on the record:**
- voice-note users (trial only);
- native apps;
- TalkBack automation;
- desktop diff review (#100911);
- publishing (no owner);
- "do this for me" behind logins, which returns only as a native Android accessibility service (date Not decided, owner Priya).

## 2. Version one: five non-negotiable features

**1. Evidence-only verdicts** (Lena, Priya, Sam).
- *Feature:*
  - Verdicts cite only DIRECT (observed) or COMPUTED (fixed-rule) lines.
  - No model call sits on the verdict path.
  - Everything else reads "never checked."
  - The verdict word is "checked," with the count.
- *Test:*
  - Every sampled verdict line resolves to a signed evidence ID.
  - With model APIs blocked, verdicts re-render byte-identical.
  - Text or an image tracing to none of her inputs blocks publishing, as with round 3's cakes.
  - One changed price yields a diff naming only that change.
  - No string says "ready."

**2. Paid blind witnesses who type what they heard** (all four).
- *Feature:*
  - Witnesses recall what they heard, never just "yes."
  - Queues are seeded with known faults.
  - Masked TEST orders use readers the harness never runs.
  - Pay is in naira every Friday.
  - Witnesses are named only by choice.
- *Test:*
  - Every witness line holds a typed transcript.
  - Pasting the visible text fails a seed whose screen says "Order" while TalkBack says "Send request."
  - A witness's first thirty checks are seeded one in three.
  - No reader supports a verdict before 200 calibration trials give it a lower bound of at least 80 percent.
  - Lines show reader, version, phone, browser, time and a per-record pseudonym.
  - Payouts land by 18:00 Friday.

**3. Recall for irreversible values** (Lena, Priya).
- *Feature:*
  - Prices and phone numbers are recalled, spoken or typed as digits, never confirmed with "yes."
  - The order number confirms an SMS code.
- *Test:*
  - A wrong price planted in "What I understood" is caught.
  - "Yes" alone confirms nothing.
  - Typed digits work everywhere.
  - An unconfirmed number cannot publish.

**4. Text first, on her phone, with a person behind it** (Lena, Priya, Yusuf).
- *Feature:*
  - No streaming, and every sound is also a line of text.
  - Alerts use rhythm, never pitch.
  - Pages reflow at 400 percent zoom.
  - There is a braille-only path.
  - Every machine step has a human fallback, including a free phone line.
- *Test:*
  - Five core tasks pass with JavaScript off at 360 px in Opera Mini and Phoenix, under 60 KB.
  - Pages reflow at 320 CSS px.
  - No default list runs over four items without a heading.
  - Two braille-only testers, one deafblind, finish onboarding, a build and the record with no sound.
  - Calls are returned within 15 minutes.

**5. Independence and refusals by instrument** (Lena, Priya, Sam).
- *Feature:*
  - The builder neither sees nor steers what scores it.
  - One public price list applies to everyone.
  - Money flows only one way.
  - Refusals are computed before publishing.
  - Nothing customer-facing says the builder is blind.
- *Test:*
  - The builder reaches the signer only through the public API.
  - Masked-page picks reproduce from the drand public randomness beacon.
  - Seeds decrypt only for the queue service and the witness lead.
  - Every planted refusal case blocks.
  - The dependence banner (Section 7) prints above a 15 percent builder share.
  - No blindness badge appears unless she adds one.

## 3. The user's experience, step by step

**Discovery.** People post the link, never a bot:
- Yusuf's sellers and groups, about 2,400 members;
- NAB Lagos and Oyo meetings, monthly from November;
- the Oshodi rehabilitation centre;
- market groups, starting with Ikorodu.

UCH Ibadan's eye clinic hands out the desk number on a card, since a newly blind patient has no screen reader yet. No referral bounties.

**First session** (Yusuf's script):
- **Minute 0:** "A person answers this number, free: ring once and we call back."
- **Minute 1:** phone number and SMS code.
- **Minute 2:** four headings: what we make, what we keep, who checks (up to two TEST orders a month), and cost: nothing.
- **Minute 4:** "Already have a catalog or a page?" If yes, we check it. If no, we make one, or route her to the catalog if October 30 says she can finish it alone.
- **Minute 5:** she dictates or types items, prices, order number and days.
- **Minute 9:** "What I understood" (five templated sentences), then "What I will add that you did not ask for: nothing."
- **Minute 11:** she recalls prices and order number (typed digits allowed); the number confirms a code.
- **Minute 13:** "Make my page," then "A blind checker usually answers within two hours, eight to nine."
- **If stuck:** after three minutes, or on "help," "Talk to a person" moves to the top.
- **Targets:** median 15 minutes; seven in ten who open the link reach "Make my page."

**Build loop.**
- Her spec fills a signed template (about 15 cents); the agent handles only what no template holds ("sold out today").
- A second vendor's model re-derives the spec from her words, and disagreements become questions to her.
- The harness runs its checks (Section 6).
- A blind witness types what TalkBack or Jieshuo said, and a sighted checker views any photo.
- The drand beacon may pick the page for a masked order.
- A Web Push or SMS buzz says the whole answer waits under a new heading.

**What she hears.**
- "Checked," with the count.
- "Everything on this page, words and pictures, is something you said, sent or approved."
- A computed map in her visual words: "the dark red Order under each price."
- "Never checked" for the rest.

The receipt stays "kept, not read" until someone asks.

**Publishing and revisions.**
- She posts the URL herself.
- The page is static on a Lagos CDN edge.
- The order button is a wa.me link with no database, so customers' numbers stay in her chat.
- A new number publishes at most three pages in 30 days.
- On Fridays, "because flour and diesel move," she hears the exact diff.
- Whether value-only edits need a fresh witness is Not decided (Lena, Yusuf).

**Help and failure.**
- The desk answers by phone, WhatsApp or voice note.
- If a customer says the button does nothing, she asks "why?" and hears which reader, phone and browser heard it, and which were "never checked."
- Harm pauses the page within 15 minutes, with an SMS to her and a desk call within 30.
- A disputed line is re-checked within four hours (4.4).
- "Delete everything" removes the page within an hour.

- **Languages:** English, Pidgin and Yoruba live. Igbo and Hausa by interpreter callback within a day.
- **Channels:**

  - If counsel finds by December 1 that Meta's terms cover this line, voice notes move to voicemail.
- **Targets:** a WhatsApp reply within 30 minutes, or by 09:00 next day. 80 percent of issues closed in a day; 95 percent in three.
- **Staff:** two blind agents from January 4, on seven-hour shifts six days a week. Each earns ₦350,000 a month plus ₦40,000 for data. A lead is named from April; one agent is added per 150 active sellers.
- **Ownership:** desk and witnesses belong to the signer; the builder buys Kemi's desk hours at list.
- **Voice-note intake (contested):** from January 11, twenty sellers come in by voice note. A blind agent types each into the same form, marked "entered by desk agent."

### 4.2 Witness network (Yusuf; seeds and cutoffs: Lena)
- **Recruitment** (by October 31):
  - thirty applicants take a paid ten-minute entry check;
  - twenty-four are trained: twenty plus four alternates;
  - all are adults with six months' daily screen-reader use, on their own phones;
  - they come from Yusuf's groups, FCE (Special) Oyo and MTN Foundation's sixty blind scholars.
  - Under-18s may build with guardian consent, never witness.
- **Training:** three paid hours:
  - type what you heard; never log in, solve CAPTCHAs or touch accounts;
  - twelve practice pages, four of them seeds;
  - privacy and payout.
- **Calibration:** November 9–29. Ten TalkBack and ten Jieshuo witnesses; 200 seeded trials per reader.
- **Scheduling:**
  - three ten-minute checks per 30-minute session;
  - at most two sessions a day, five a week;
  - 4 to 15 checks a week (₦45,000 at most);
  - random assignment, no swaps;
  - relatives' businesses declared.
- **Per 100 builds:** about 135 checks, seeds included.
  - Six active blind witnesses (four TalkBack, two Jieshuo) at about 22 checks a month.
  - Plus a sighted photo checker and two paid reserves.
  - Priya's model: 7 at 100 builds, 27 at 1,000, about 210 at 10,000.
- **Pay:**
  - ₦3,000 per phone check;
  - ₦4,500 per masked order;
  - seeds paid as checks;
  - ₦1,500 per sighted photo check;
  - ₦22,500 per professional full check.
- **Payout:**
  - Fridays by 18:00, by instant transfer to her own account or wallet (mostly OPay, PalmPay, Moniepoint), fee ours.
  - Account number typed twice, name read back; no BVN.
  - A payout seven days late closes seller sign-up automatically.
- **Quality and fraud:**
  - Seeds come one in three for the first thirty checks, then one in ten.
  - Four misses in ten bring paid retraining.
  - Six misses in the last ten seeds bring suspension (if honest misses run near one in ten): about twice per thousand witness-years for an honest witness, within eight seeds for a non-listener.
  - Her lines void back to her last caught seed, with free re-checks for anyone who relied on them.
  - First suspension 30 days; second permanent. Clawback of pay: Not decided (Yusuf).
  - One witness per phone number and payout account.
  - Seed wording rotates weekly.
  - Two human reviewers, one from the council, decide within seven days, after a phone hearing.
- **Empty queues:**
  - January's roughly 140 checks (sponsor pool, twenty from developers, about ten from Count Us) employ seven witnesses.
  - The other thirteen get a paid monthly session (₦9,000), first call on Canary and Lena's study, and the truth: "Your queue is empty because we have fifty sellers, not because you failed."
  - At 500 builds a month, about thirty witnesses earn about $45 a month each: "a supplement, said plainly."
- **Witness lead:** one of the twenty; half-time from January, ₦200,000 a month; holds the seeds.

### 4.3 Moderation (Lena; the rota sits at Yusuf's desk)
- Refusals run before publishing (Section 11).
- Borderline requests are held for a duty reviewer, who rules within 24 hours.
  - The rota is the agent on shift, the desk lead and a part-time sighted image reviewer, at least two of them blind.
  - Appeals go to three council members, who decide within seven days.
- Pages dressed as a bank, telco, agency, charity or disability organisation are held. "MTN recharge sold here" passes.
- The image reviewer's pay is Not decided (Yusuf).

### 4.4 Incidents (Priya)

| Class | Trigger | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Sev 1 (class one) | Orders to an unconfirmed number; unapproved content; refusal hit; money lost; tampering | Automatic pause and signed revocation within 1 minute, or within 15 minutes of a human report. Seller told at once by SMS and in the top line of "What changed." Desk call within 30 minutes. Random re-check by a witness not told it is a dispute |
| Sev 2 (class two) | Disputed line | Re-check by another witness within 4 hours; public correction within 48 |
| Sev 3 | Bad template or check class | Lines revoked within 1 hour; pages re-checked within 48 |
| Breach | Personal data | Data Protection Commission within 72 hours |

- One anonymous report never pauses a page.
- Two reports from different numbers within an hour can pause it, as can a group admin's flagger number.
- Hourly re-crawls treat any unsigned change as tampering.

### 4.5 Rhythm
- **Daily:** desk, witness sessions, re-crawls, on-call.
- **Tuesday:** release. TalkBack and Jieshuo testers report within 24 hours; the blind release lead decides.
- **Friday:** edits and diffs; payouts by 18:00.
- **Weekly:** Lena's dashboard; seed rewording; Canary's three rows. Day, attendees and Canary's runner are Not decided (Lena).
- **Monthly:** council by dial-in; practice sessions; braille-only, iPhone VoiceOver and zoom testers; NAB demonstrations.
- **Quarterly:** public report.

## 11. Legal, safety and compliance (Sam; consent and refusals: Lena)

This is Sam's reading, not counsel's.

**Nigeria.**
- **Companies:** two not-for-profit companies registered at the Corporate Affairs Commission by December 18.
- **Data protection:**
  - registration with the Data Protection Commission, under the 2023 Act and 2025 directive;
  - explicit consent and an impact assessment, because disability status is sensitive data;
  - disclosure that Kemi's words go to US model servers, a cross-border transfer;
  - breach reports within 72 hours.
- **Payouts:** an accountant clears witness pay under the 2025 Tax Act before the first payout. Weekly payouts stay under the CBN's ₦50,000 Tier 1 limit, so no BVN is needed; Sam calls this "a 2017 figure still repeated in 2026 guides."
- **Consumer protection:** we say "checked," never "safe."
- **Count Us:**
  - the university runs it;
  - banks get results 30 days early, a printed right of reply and no veto;
  - counsel reviews it first, because section 24 of the Cybercrimes Act still reaches critics.
- **WhatsApp:** no bot and no Business API. Counsel rules on the human line by December 1.

**European Union (the paid door).**
- Business-to-business only, with VAT numbers.
- A GDPR Article 27 representative.
- AI Act Article 50 makes the INFERRED tag machine-readable.
- The Product Liability Directive covers software from December 9 but excludes business economic loss, so contract caps govern.
- The Platform Work Directive, due December 2, requires human review of every witness suspension. We apply this everywhere.

**Signed records.**
- A signed line is an observation and a time, never a warranty.
- Liability is capped at twelve months of fees.
- Errors get a public correction within 48 hours.
- Witnesses are never personally liable.
- The indemnity quote is pending (Sam).

**Consent and privacy.**
- Before the first build, she hears four headings of at most four items each.
- Masked TEST orders, at most two a month, can be declined. The record then says "not in the outside check," and the opt-out rate is published.
- Nothing customer-facing says the builder is blind.
- Each witness has two name switches, both off by default: "first name to the seller" and "first name on the public record."
- Work and research consent are separate; declining research changes neither work nor pay.
- **Count Us participants:**
  - consent by teach-back;
  - are paid cash, finished or not;
  - never share a PIN or login, or press confirm.
  - We keep typed transcripts, never banking screenshots.

**Refused before publishing:**
- fields asking for a PIN, password, one-time code, card number, CVV, BVN or NIN;
- pages collecting or holding other people's money: donations, savings groups, investments, "returns";
- claims to cure disease, blindness included. Lena cites Kuriyan and colleagues (NEJM, 2017): three women blinded by unproven "stem cell" injections.
- order links to unconfirmed numbers.

Witnesses never solve CAPTCHAs, log in or act on anyone's account, and CAPTCHAs never reach the paid network: "a paid CAPTCHA queue is a bot farm" (Priya).
